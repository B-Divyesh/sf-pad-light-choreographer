LEGAL SHEET / 01

Privacy

Effective 27 August 2026. Pad Light Choreographer is local-first and has no account, analytics, advertisements, or tracking pixels.

What stays on your device

Your routines and MIDI preferences are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. MIDI messages are processed in the page and are not recorded or transmitted. Export happens only when you ask for a JSON download.

Network use

After the first visit, the installed app can work offline. The hosting provider may receive ordinary server request logs such as IP address and user agent for security and reliability; this product does not add identifiers or combine those logs into profiles.

Your control

Delete individual routines in the app or clear this site’s storage in browser settings. Uninstalling may not clear site storage automatically. You may inspect or carry your data with “Export JSON.”

Contact

Questions can be raised through the project repository linked by the site operator.
