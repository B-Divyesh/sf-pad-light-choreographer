Privacy

Effective 6 September 2026. Pad Light Choreographer has no account, ads, analytics, or tracking pixels.

What stays on your device

Your routines and MIDI preferences are stored in your browser’s IndexedDB. MIDI messages are processed in the page. The app does not record or transmit them. Export happens only when you choose a JSON download.

Network use

After the first visit, the installed app can work offline. The hosting provider may receive ordinary server logs for security and reliability. This product does not add identifiers or build profiles.

Your control

Delete routines in the app or clear this site’s storage in browser settings. Uninstalling may not clear site storage. Export JSON when you want a copy of a routine.

Contact

Questions can be raised in the project repository.
